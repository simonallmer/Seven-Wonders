Palace — “The Span”

PROTOTYPE
This is a shape-and-pool sandbox, not the finished game. Set stones to feel out the board; movement comes next.

THE BOARD
An elongated stepped pyramid, 15 long × 5 deep. The cross-section rises L1 → L2 → L3 → L2 → L1. A transept crosses at the centre, lifting that line to the top — so the highest level forms an elongated + (the long nave ridge crossed by the short transept).

RUN
Slide along one level in a straight line, as far as you like, until a wall, a stone or the edge stops you.

JUMP
Hop to an adjacent field exactly one level higher or lower. Never two levels — so a two-high step is an impassable wall. That is how you build traps.

SHATTER
Destroy a whole glass block. You must be next to the block you break, or you may shatter the block beneath your own feet — in which case you drop straight down to the ground and stand upright. Next turn you can run through the opening.

NO RESTING INSIDE
You can only ever stand on an open surface, never enclosed within the building — pass through, don't dwell.
