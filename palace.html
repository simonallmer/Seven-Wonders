Palace — “The Span”

PROTOTYPE
Race your stones across the Crystal Palace. First stone to the opposite end wins.

THE BOARD
The nave runs 7 columns from west to east, 5 rows deep. 7 White stones start on the south row, 7 Black on the north row. The race: White to the west edge (x=0), Black to the east edge (x=6).

RUN
Roll in a straight line across the nave in any of the four cardinal directions. Stop when you reach the edge, a friendly stone blocks you, or land on an enemy to capture it.

CAPTURE
Roll directly onto an enemy stone and it's removed from the board. Clear a path to the far end to win.

THE BUILDING
The glass structure is purely decorative — it does not affect movement. Race through the open spaces between the columns.
