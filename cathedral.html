Cathedral — “The Pilgrimage”

PROTOTYPE
Core sandbox. Mirror-equal, perfect information. Win condition still being designed.

LEVELS & STAIRS
You stand on a level (−1, 0, +1). The two side stairs (attached at the middle of the left/right edges) are the only place to change level: step on, then pick Down / Normal / Up to come back onto the board at that level — your reset/elevator.

THE TERRAIN
The board is a chessboard. It starts flat. Every field (except the two starts and the rose) can be Raised (+1) or Lowered (−1) — but White may only lift white fields, Black only black. Your fields are the diagonals, so you build alone, but you cross your opponent's walls by raising your own to match.

MOVE
Step to an adjacent field at the same height or lower — you can never climb up. A raised field is a wall; a lowered field is a pit you fall into and must work your way out of.

YOUR TURN — one action
Move one step, or Raise / Lower one of your own-colour fields (↑/↓ keys). You may never reshape the field you stand on — so you can't simply rise out of a trap.

THE CATCH
Your colour is your toolkit: raise it into walls, sink it into pits, to maze and trap your opponent on the squares they must cross — while you race the same gauntlet they build for you.
