# Marketing images

All 1920 x 1080 (16:9), photographed from the running game with no menus.

- `hall.jpg` — the Exhibition, down the length of the table. `hall-logo.jpg` is the same shot with the Seven Wonders symbol and title in the middle. `hall-side.jpg` is a second angle from beside the table. `hall-fountain.jpg` looks past the crystal fountain to the lily canal and the elm. `hall-title.jpg`, `hall-side-title.jpg` and `hall-fountain-title.jpg` carry the title alone, without the symbol. The `-empty` versions (`hall-empty.jpg`, `hall-side-empty.jpg`, `hall-fountain-empty.jpg`) show the same views without the visitors. The empty side view, turned a little left so the fire engine and the skyscraper sit fully in frame, also comes at three times of day: `hall-side-empty-dawn.jpg`, `hall-side-empty-day.jpg` and `hall-side-empty-night.jpg`.
- One in-game image per playable wonder, each a position some moves into a game: `pyramid`, `gardens`, `temple`, `statue`, `mausoleum`, `colossus`, `pharos`, `great-wall`, `cathedral`, `skyscraper` (the last at dusk, the others in afternoon light).
- Three more views, each at dawn, midday and night (`-dawn`, `-day`, `-night`), no title: `hall-front-*` straight down the table, `hall-top-*` from directly above, and `hall-low-*` a low telephoto view along the table past the Skyscraper.
