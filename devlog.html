Seven Wonders · Allmer Games — Concept Vault

CONFIDENTIAL · INTERNAL

House rules of the series

Every Seven Wonders game is simple, abstract, deep, and must earn its place by the Only Original principle — it draws on a timeless game-family but contributes a new symbol that only its wonder could license.

  • Never asymmetric. Both sides always play by the same rules with mirror-equal forces. (Rules out the pursuit / Tafl / Fox-&-Geese family entirely.)
  • The soul is the design logic. Theme dresses the mechanic; it never replaces it. A material (glass, stone, die) only belongs if it changes a rule.

The panopticum — families already mined

✓ Territory / surround — Mausoleum, Colossus, Colosseum
✓ Move & jump — Temple, Great Wall
✓ Stacking — Gardens
✓ Slide & push — Mausoleum, Colossus
✓ Value pieces (dice/number) — Statue, Tower
✓ Line / set completion — Skyscraper, Tower
✓ Light / projection (our invention) — Pharos, Skyscraper
✗ Asymmetry — forbidden by house rule

Untapped — the open frontier

○ Connection (Hex / TwixT) → claimed by PALACE
○ Sowing / growth (mancala) → reserved for CITADEL
○ Tile / path laying → candidate for CATHEDRAL

Note: Gardens' Trail already grazes sowing (dropping one stone per field from a moving stack) — so Citadel must take the full "plant & grow over time" version to stay original.

LOCKED

Palace — “The Span”

after the Crystal Palace (1851) · felt location: a hall of glass and light · the modern wing

Family: Connection — the series' first. Why this wonder: the Crystal Palace is the only wonder that is fundamentally a modular kit that spans a distance (Paxton's prefab glass panes + the axial nave & transept). No other wonder licenses connection without borrowing.

Core

  • The board is the plan: a long nave crossed at the centre by the transept arch. Cells are glass bays.
  • Each turn, lay one glass pane. Black spans the nave end-to-end (west↔east); White spans across the transept (north↔south). First unbroken chain wins.
  • The new symbol — fragility: glass left unsupported by neighbours shatters and is removed. You don't just connect; you connect something that holds. (Repurposes Mausoleum's Collapse from capture into structural tension.)
  • The transept is the shared keystone both spans must cross.

Open: board proportions (elongation vs. balance), exact support/shatter rule, whether the transept is a single keystone cell or a band. See the live field (Palace, password-gated).

RESERVED

Citadel — “Growth”

after Machu Picchu · felt location: the terraced mountain

Family: Sowing / growth (mancala lineage) — the full version Gardens only hinted at.

Seed idea (Simon's): plant something, and something grows

  • You don't place finished pieces — you plant seeds, and on later turns they grow on their own (spread to adjacent terraces / rise a level / yield a new stone). Time is a player.
  • Machu Picchu's agricultural terraces are the board — stepped levels where growth flows downhill or climbs, and where what you sow shapes what you can later reap.
  • Possible Only-Original twist: growth is deterministic and visible (like Frontier turning luck into certainty) — you can read the future of the field and must plan around the harvest, not gamble on it.

Open: what exactly "grows" (count, height, spread), and the win condition (most harvest? control of terraces?). Keep distinct from Gardens (opaque stacking) and from sowing-as-mere-distribution.

OPEN

Cathedral — brainstorm

after the great Gothic cathedral · felt location: TBD (keep abstract — do NOT drift into Detective Noname's Victorian London)

Remaining untapped family best suited here: tile / path-laying (you build the structure as you play). Two candidate directions:

  • Vault-raising (tile-laying): players lay rib/arch tiles to raise the vaulted ceiling bay by bay; a vault only stands if its arches meet and balance (thrust must be answered by counter-thrust) — the Gothic engineering principle as the rule. Score completed vaults.
  • Symmetry / the mirror: the cathedral is bilaterally symmetric (nave) and radially symmetric (rose window). A game where a move must be answered in mirror, or where you score by completing symmetric figures, would be a genuinely new abstract primitive. Untested — high originality, needs prototyping.

Avoid: stained-glass light as the core — that collides with Pharos / Skyscraper. Light may appear only as dressing.

Magic is Real — the human element here is the design logic itself. Guard it.
