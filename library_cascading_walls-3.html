Game Overview (7x7 Board):

• Lay down field plates to create a walkable path, or place walls to block opponents.

• Only ONE starting plate is placed in the exact middle of each player's starting line.

• Slide/push walls when multiple are connected, shifting the maze dynamically.

• Topple a wall onto adjacent spaces. If there's no plate there, the fallen wall instantly becomes a new plate! Crushes any player's stone in its way.

How to Play Dominoverse

Goal: Reach any of the 7 fields on your opposite side of the board before your opponents do.

Game Setup: The 7x7 board starts empty except for exactly one starting field plate placed in the middle of each player's side. Players start off-board and must deploy onto their starting plate to begin.

Your Actions (Choose 1 per turn):

  • Lay Plate / Wall: Click any empty cell block to lay a walkable Plate, OR click any empty grid-gap line to place a Wall. Note: No starting line boundaries can be walled to avoid trapping players.
  • Move Figure: Move your stone 1 cell orthogonally. You can only move onto placed Field Plates, and you cannot cross any standing walls.
  • Push Wall: Slide a standing wall by 1 space. If other walls are connected to it in the push direction, they slide along too (propagation), provided there is an empty gap at the end to accommodate the shift.
  • Topple Wall (Domino Smash): Select a wall and a fall direction. The wall collapses flat onto the adjacent fields. Any fallen wall block automatically becomes a walkable plate (even if the space was completely empty before!). Any player stone on those fields is crushed and returned to their reserve off-board. Parallel adjacent walls continue the chain reaction!
